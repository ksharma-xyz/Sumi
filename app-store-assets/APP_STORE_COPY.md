## Description
*(paste as-is into App Store Connect — plain text only, no markdown)*

```
墨 Sumi is sudoku stripped of noise.

No leaderboards. No streaks that shame you. No timers ticking in your face. Just a nine-by-nine grid, rendered in warm ink on washi paper, waiting for your next move.


FIVE LEVELS OF PRACTICE

Easy through Edo — five difficulties that take you from your first quiet solve to the edge of mastery.

· Easy / Medium — unhurried, forgiving.
· Hard — where focus begins to matter.
· Master / Edo — for those who have earned stillness.

Each level is a different conversation with the grid.


A DAILY HABIT

Open Sumi each day. Solve one puzzle. Watch your streak grow and your times sharpen. The Practice Log tracks your progress with a clean improvement curve — no gamification, just the graph.


YOUR SEASON, YOUR PALETTE

Choose a season at first launch — Spring, Summer, Autumn, or Winter. Each shifts the accent colour that breathes through the interface. Change it anytime in Settings.


A MOMENT OF REST

Pause anytime. The grid fades. A kanji appears. A line of prose.

"A moment of rest. Your time is paused. Return when you are ready."

No guilt. No penalty. Just stillness.


SUMI PRO

Unlock the full practice:

· The Library — a curated collection of passages from Zen masters, philosophers, and makers. One surfaces each day.
· Design Zen Sudoku — build a print-ready A5 puzzle book. Pick your difficulty mix, your paper colour, your ink. Export as PDF and print.
· Full improvement history and personal bests at every difficulty.
· No ads, ever.

One-time purchase. No subscription. No renewal.


ABOUT THE NAME

墨 (sumi) is the ink used in traditional Japanese calligraphy and sumi-e painting — ground from a solid inkstick, mixed with water, applied with patience. The name describes the aesthetic: deliberate, unhurried, lasting.


Ink on paper. No chrome. No noise.
Nine by nine, every day.
```
