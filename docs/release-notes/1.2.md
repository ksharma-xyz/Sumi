# Sumi 1.2 - App Store "What's New"

Paste-ready copy for the App Store / Play listing. Plain language, no markdown
needed in the store field. Copy rule: no middle dot, no em dash.

## Short (one-liner, for tight fields)

Notes that stay put, smarter hints, a clearer board, and new ways to play.

## Standard (recommended)

This update makes Sumi calmer to read and smarter to play.

Your pencil marks are now saved with the puzzle, so they survive closing the
app, and they tidy themselves up as you place numbers.

The board is clearer too: larger numbers that stay a fixed, readable size, a
roomier grid, and stronger lines around the nine boxes.

New in this version:
- Smarter hints that point you to a square you can actually work out next
- Long-press Notes to auto-fill every empty square with its candidates
- Redo, to step forward again after an undo
- High-legibility numerals, for a crisper typeface
- Conflicts-only highlighting, so guesses are not spoiled
- Digit-first input: tap a number, then tap squares to place it
- Lives mode, for a tougher challenge
- The timer pauses when you leave and freezes while paused, and the board
  reopens right where you left off

Thanks for playing. More calm puzzles ahead.
