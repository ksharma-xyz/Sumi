# Sumi 1.3 - App Store "What's New"

Paste-ready copy for the App Store / Play listing. Plain language, no markdown
needed in the store field. Copy rule: no middle dot, no em dash.

Note: these notes restate the 1.2 highlights alongside the 1.3 work, so they
cover everything since 1.1.

## Short (one-liner, for tight fields)

A clearer board, smarter hints, saved pencil marks, and a game that always
picks up right where you left off.

## Standard (recommended)

This update makes Sumi calmer to read and smarter to play.

Your progress is safe now. The game you were playing is always waiting for
you, even days later, exactly as you left it. Pencil marks are saved with the
puzzle and tidy themselves up as you place numbers.

The board is clearer too: larger numbers that stay a fixed, readable size,
bigger and neater pencil marks, a roomier grid, and stronger lines around the
nine boxes.

And it feels better in the hand: the board blooms onto the screen like ink on
paper, puzzles load faster, and resuming a game is instant.

New in this version:
- Smarter hints that point you to a square you can actually work out next
- Long-press Notes to auto-fill every empty square with its candidates
- Redo, to step forward again after an undo
- High-legibility numerals, for a crisper typeface
- Conflicts-only highlighting, so guesses are not spoiled
- Digit-first input: tap a number, then tap squares to place it
- Lives mode, for a tougher challenge
- The timer pauses when you leave and freezes while paused

Thanks for playing. More calm puzzles ahead.
