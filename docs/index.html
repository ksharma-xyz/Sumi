Sumi

A quiet sudoku practice.

Ink on paper. Nothing in the way. Sumi is a daily Sudoku app designed around stillness — no leaderboards, no nagging notifications, no celebration confetti. Just the grid, warm paper underneath, and the quiet satisfaction of placing the last number.

Empty space is not empty. It is waiting.
House — Sumi quote book
壱 The practice
日

Daily

One puzzle a day, the same for everyone — generated deterministically from the date so the difficulty curve is shared.

静

Stillness

No leaderboards, no nagging notifications, no "you broke your record!" overlays. Skip a day, skip a week — the puzzle waits.

記

Record

Quiet stats. Personal bests by difficulty, an improvement curve, a calendar of the days you played. Nothing more.

墨

Aesthetic

Warm washi paper, soft ink tones, an editorial serif typeface, and Japanese typographic accents. Sudoku as it should look.
